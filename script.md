# 🎬 PricePilot — Loom Demo Video Script (2.5 – 3 Minutes)

> **Goal:** Showcase PricePilot as an autonomous, margin-protected competitive intelligence & dynamic repricing engine for e-commerce brands and sellers. Keep it crisp, visual, and high-impact.

---

## 📋 Pre-Recording Checklist & Setup
1. **Tabs to keep open:**
   - Tab 1: `http://localhost:3000` (PricePilot Dashboard)
2. **Pre-populated Data:**
   - 2–3 products in Catalogue (e.g., boAt Airdopes 311 Pro).
   - At least 1 competitor tracked with a verified price.
   - Repricing view open with a pending recommendation.
   - AI Insights & Copilot with history ready.

## 🎙️ Step-by-Step Script & Actions

### 1. Hook & Introduction (0:00 – 0:20)
**🖥️ Screen:** PricePilot Dashboard (`Product Catalogue` view).  
**🗣️ What to Say:**  
> *"Hi everyone! Today, e-commerce sellers face a huge dilemma: competitors change prices multiple times a day. If you don't react, you lose sales; but if you drop prices blindly, you sell at a loss and destroy your margins.*  
>  
> *Meet **PricePilot** — an autonomous, margin-protected competitive intelligence and intelligent dynamic repricing platform built to solve this exact problem."*

---

### 2. Product Catalogue & Mathematical Margin Protection (0:20 – 0:45)
**🖥️ Screen:** Click on **Product Catalogue**. Hover over Cost Price, Selling Price, and Min Margin %.  
**🗣️ What to Say:**  
> *"It starts with the **Product Catalogue**. For every product, we define the cost price, current selling price, and a non-negotiable **Minimum Gross Margin percentage**.*  
>  
> *PricePilot mathematically calculates a hard floor price using:*  
> `Floor Price = Cost Price / (1 - Min Margin %)`  
> *No recommendation will ever drop below this floor, guaranteeing you never sell at a loss."*

---

### 3. Competitor Discovery & Gemini AI Matching (0:45 – 1:15)
**🖥️ Screen:** Click **Competitor Tracking** ➔ Click **Discover Competitors** (modal opens with candidates).  
**🗣️ What to Say:**  
> *"Next is **Competitor Tracking**. Finding real competitor listings across Google Shopping, Blinkit, or Amazon is tricky because of wrong variants and accessories.*  
>  
> *PricePilot solves this with **Google Gemini AI** matching: it automatically classifies listings into **Exact Match**, **Variant Match**, or blocks **Accessories & Mismatches** with a confidence score and explanation.*  
>  
> *We can verify live prices directly from the retailer's page with our built-in direct scraper."*

---

### 4. Automated Price Monitoring & Real-Time Alerts (1:15 – 1:40)
**🖥️ Screen:** Point to the top **Auto-Recheck** button, then click the **Notification Bell** icon in the header.  
**🗣️ What to Say:**  
> *"PricePilot runs a background monitoring engine that automatically tracks all active competitors on a configurable schedule.*  
>  
> *With our clean **Auto-Recheck** button, we can trigger instant updates anytime. When competitor prices drop or jump, our change-detection system creates real-time notifications with percentage and price differences without alerting on false positives."*

---

### 5. Margin-Protected Dynamic Repricing Engine (1:40 – 2:15)
**🖥️ Screen:** Navigate to **Dynamic Repricing** tab. Show pending recommendations, strategy dropdown, and Click **Approve**.  
**🗣️ What to Say:**  
> *"Now the core engine: **Dynamic Repricing**.*  
> *Here, PricePilot analyzes verified competitor prices against configurable strategies — like Match Lowest, Undercut by ₹10, or Premium Pricing.*  
>  
> *Notice this indicator: if a competitor prices at ₹700, but our margin floor is ₹750, the engine **refuses to drop below ₹750** and clearly explains why.*  
>  
> *Every change requires human approval: with one click, I can **Approve** or **Reject** the recommendation, instantly recording an auditable pricing history."*

---

### 6. AI Pricing Insights & AI Copilot (2:15 – 2:40)
**🖥️ Screen:** Click **AI Pricing Insights** (show cards), then open **AI Pricing Copilot** (type a quick prompt or click a starter chip).  
**🗣️ What to Say:**  
> *"Finally, we have enterprise AI intelligence built-in:*  
> 1. ***AI Pricing Insights***: *Gemini scans our entire catalog to highlight underpriced items, margin risks, and competitor moves in plain English.*  
> 2. ***AI Pricing Copilot***: *An interactive pricing assistant that executes live database tools to answer complex questions like 'Which products are priced higher than competitors?' or provide instant margin audits."*

---

### 7. Tech Stack & Conclusion (2:40 – 2:55)
**🖥️ Screen:** Zoom out to the full sleek PricePilot UI.  
**🗣️ What to Say:**  
> *"PricePilot is built with **Next.js 14**, **Node.js/Express**, **MongoDB Atlas**, and the official **Google Gemini 3.8 Flash SDK** with zero external frontend UI bloat and 100% test coverage across our pricing math.*  
>  
> *PricePilot gives e-commerce sellers automated agility while safeguarding their profits. Thanks for watching!"*

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for a 10/10 Loom Video:
- **Tone:** Confident, professional, and fast-paced.
- **Pointers:** Use mouse cursor circles or smooth movements — avoid rapid jerky mouse shakes.
- **Language:** If speaking in Hinglish, you can seamlessly replace English phrases with natural Hindi (e.g., *"Yahan humara margin floor ensure karta hai ki hum kabhi loss me na bechein"*).
- **Time Check:** If you run over 3 minutes, trim the AI Copilot query demo to just clicking a pre-made chip.
